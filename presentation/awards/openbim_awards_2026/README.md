# buildingSMART openBIM Awards 2026

## ENMA-WG Submission

**Category:** Professional Research

**Submission title:**  
Bridging OpenBIM Knowledge for Building Services Engineering

This directory contains materials prepared by ENMA-WG for the
buildingSMART openBIM Awards 2026.

The video `scope_of_the_entry.mp4` was created as part of the submission
and provides an overview of the scope and concept of the ENMA-WG activities.

The submission was not selected as a finalist.

## Purpose of Publication

The video is published here as a record of the ENMA-WG research and
development process and as background material for subsequent PoC
development.

The work continues toward the buildingSMART International Summit Tokyo 2026.
